# Executive Sales Dashboard \| Power BI

An interactive sales analytics dashboard built with **Microsoft Power
BI** to explore revenue, gross profit, profitability, order volume,
product-category performance, sales-channel contribution, and
country-level sales performance.

This is my first end-to-end interactive dashboard project. I developed
it while learning through a combination of online tutorials, hands-on
practice, and experimentation. The project helped me apply DAX
calculations, KPI design, data modeling concepts, trend analysis, and
data visualization in a practical setting.

## Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes headline KPI cards, interactive slicers, trend
analysis, ranked country comparisons, sales-channel contribution,
product-category performance, and a geographic view.

## Key Insights

-   **Country performance:** The United Kingdom is the highest-revenue
    country shown, at approximately **74K**, followed by Germany at
    **70K** and France at **55K**.
-   **Sales-channel contribution:** The Online Store contributes
    approximately **119.04K**, or **40.15%**, of the channel revenue
    represented in the dashboard.
-   **Product-category performance:** Hiking leads the displayed
    categories with approximately **39.19K**, followed by Camping at
    **37.69K**.
-   **Profitability overview:** The dashboard reports **296.38K** in
    gross profit and a **45.94%** gross margin.
-   **Time-based analysis:** The monthly revenue and gross-profit visual
    helps users compare performance across the displayed months. Month
    sorting and date relationships should be validated before drawing
    conclusions about chronological trends.

These observations describe the values currently visible in the
dashboard. They are descriptive findings, not proof of the causes behind
performance differences.

## Interactive Features

-   Slicers for **Month**, **Quarter**, **Product Categories**, and
    **Country**
-   KPI cards for revenue, gross profit, gross margin, quantity, and
    orders
-   Monthly revenue and gross-profit comparison
-   Top-five and bottom-five country revenue comparisons
-   Sales-channel revenue contribution chart
-   Product-category revenue chart
-   Geographic visualization of country-level performance

## How to View the Project

1.  Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2.  Open it in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3.  Use the slicers to explore the report by month, quarter, product
    category, and country.
